How to create your store

Complete these steps inside the Koyden app.

1. Create your account

Sign up with your email address, create a password, and verify your email with the code sent to you.

2. Choose “Sell products”

When asked how you want to use the app, select “Sell products” to activate the seller experience.

3. Open your profile

Go to the Profile tab, then tap “Edit Store” to start setting up your public store page.

4. Complete your store details

Add your store name, address, profile image, description, opening hours, payment methods and delivery options.

5. Save your store

Review your information carefully, then save the form to make your store profile ready for buyers.

6. Add your first product

Tap the “+” button in your products section to add photos, product details, price and availability.

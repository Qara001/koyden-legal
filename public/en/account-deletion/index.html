Account Deletion Request

Koyden allows users to request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to request account deletion

To request deletion of your Koyden account, please send an email to support@koyden.app using the email address associated with your account.

Please use the subject line: Account Deletion Request.

In your message, include:


  • your account email address

  • your full name if available on the account

  • any additional information that helps us identify your account

What happens after your request

Once we verify your request, we will process the deletion of your account and associated personal data within a reasonable period, in accordance with applicable law.

Data that will be deleted

When your deletion request is processed, we will delete or anonymize the personal data associated with your account, which may include:


  • account profile information

  • store profile information

  • product listings and related product content

  • account-related contact information

  • other personal data associated with normal use of the app

Data that may be retained

Some information may be retained where necessary:


  • to comply with legal obligations

  • to resolve disputes

  • to enforce our agreements

  • for security, fraud prevention, or abuse prevention

  • in backup systems for a limited retention period

Retention period

If retention is required for legal, security, or operational reasons, the retained data will be kept only for as long as necessary for those purposes and then securely deleted or anonymized.

Contact

If you have questions about account deletion, contact us at support@koyden.app.
